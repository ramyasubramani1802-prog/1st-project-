Our story

About Studio Soulkadi

Studio Soulkadi was born from a simple love — the joy of making something beautiful with your hands and putting a smile on someone's face. What started as a hobby of crafting tiny, satisfying fidget clickers grew into a full creative studio dedicated to handcrafted, functional, and completely quirky items.

Every item we create is 100% handcrafted with love, from the careful assembly of each letter tile to the thoughtful packaging we send your order in. We believe everyday objects should bring you joy — and that's exactly what we pour into every single piece.

Yes! Most products are fully customizable. Use our live customizer or DM us on Instagram/WhatsApp.
